Privacy

WHAT WE COLLECT AND WHY.

Emails and messages. When you join the waitlist, the signal list, or send a story or artwork, we receive the details you enter (email, product you chose, your message and any file). We use them to notify you about releases and to review community entries. We do not sell them.

Processing. Form entries are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit. Nothing you send is published without your explicit permission.

Cookies and measurement. If you press Accept, we load Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity (heatmaps and anonymised session recordings) and Microsoft Advertising to measure which pages and ads work. If you press Decline, none of them load. You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Your rights. You can ask us to delete your data or remove anything you sent by replying to any email from us.

Orders. Products are not yet on sale. Nothing on this site takes payment. When checkout opens, payments will be handled by the payment provider named at that step, and this page will be updated.

Draft policy. Have it reviewed before you rely on it for regulated markets.
